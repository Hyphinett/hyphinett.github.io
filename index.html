Bio

Hi I'm Hyphinett!

welcome to hyphin.net!

I'm a game developer, musician and artist.
Notable game titles I've worked on include If Found, Bubbo: Adventure On Geralds Island (Dread X Collection 3), and The Haunted PS1 Demo Discs (2020/21).
In addition to helping organise the Haunted PS1 Demo Discs in 2021 and 2020 I am also the founder of the Haunted PS1, having started the discord server in 2018.
I love making electronic music, in 2023 I released Spaceship Submarine, a collection of dreamy DNB tracks salvaged from an accidentally formatted hard drive, you can find it in the music section of the page.

Contact: contact@hyphin.net
